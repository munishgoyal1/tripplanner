# Welcome to the Trip Planner ✈️

This is an AI-powered trip planning assistant. Tell me where you want to go
and I'll search **real flights** (Duffel), **hotels & activities** (Amadeus),
**ratings & reviews** (Google Places) and **fresh travel content** (Tavily) to
build you a complete, bookable itinerary.

## Try these prompts

- *"Plan a 5-day family trip to Goa in January for 2 adults and 1 child (age 8)"*
- *"Find flights from Delhi to Bali for 2 adults on 2026-03-15, returning 2026-03-22"*
- *"What are the best activities in Paris with kids?"*
- *"Save my preferences: budget moderate, prefer direct flights, vegetarian"*

## Notes

- This is a **public beta** — anyone with the URL can chat. Don't share
  sensitive details.
- Booking is in **test mode** (Duffel sandbox) — no real charges, no real
  flights confirmed.
- Your preferences and trip drafts are saved against your browser session.
